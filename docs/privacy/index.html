Hermes Conduit

Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

This policy explains how Hermes Conduit handles information in the iOS app, the optional Hermes Conduit Notifier plugin, and the optional notification relay used to deliver Apple Push Notification service (APNs) alerts.

Summary

  • Conduit has no advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.
  • Your conversations, attachments, model settings, and gateway credentials are sent to the Hermes dashboard or gateway that you configure, not to a hosted Hermes service operated by Conduit.
  • The optional notification relay processes only the information needed to pair a gateway and deliver the notification categories you enable.
  • Optional Apple Health and location sharing go from your device directly to your own gateway, encrypted end to end, and never through a server operated by Conduit.
  • Personal information is not sold.

Information stored on your device

Conduit stores the dashboard URL you provide, dashboard session information, app preferences, notification registration credentials, locally selected profile avatars, and cached attachment information on your device. Sensitive session and notification credentials are stored using iOS secure storage where supported.

Your Hermes dashboard and gateway

When you sign in, send a message, load a session, change a model or setting, run a command, or upload an attachment, Conduit communicates with the dashboard and gateway URL that you configured. The operator of that Hermes installation controls the storage, processing, retention, and security of that data. If you connect to a gateway operated by someone else, their privacy terms also apply.

Optional push notifications

If you enable notifications, Conduit sends the following information to the Conduit notification relay:

  • Your APNs device token and a randomly generated installation identifier.
  • Notification preferences, such as enabled event categories, sound, and preview settings.
  • A gateway display name and randomly generated, revocable pairing credentials.
  • For notification delivery: event type, event identifier, session identifier, profile name, gateway identifier, and limited notification title and body text supplied by your gateway. This text can reach the relay even when notification previews are disabled, unless end-to-end encryption is on for that pairing (see below).
  • For approval and clarify cards: the routing identifier (the session identifier used as an approval session key, or a clarify request ID), approval description or clarify questions, available choice labels, and, for clarify responses, the answers you submit.
  • Basic request metadata used for security, rate limiting, diagnostics, and abuse prevention, which may include IP address, request path, response status, and time.

Notification previews control the title and body shown in the APNs alert; they do not control what the plugin sends to the relay. Decision cards have a separate preference, enabled by default, that controls structured decision content in the APNs payload. Turning off previews or decision cards does not prevent that content from reaching the relay.

To limit content before it leaves your Hermes gateway, enable the plugin's profile-specific redaction setting with hermes conduit-push redact on. Redaction removes notification title and body text and replaces approval descriptions and clarify questions with generic text. Routing identifiers still reach the relay. Choice labels also remain for both card types: approval labels are limited to the fixed once, session, always, and deny vocabulary, while clarify labels remain as supplied. Answers you submit for clarify cards are sent to the relay for the gateway to retrieve, even when redaction is enabled.

When end-to-end encryption is on for a pairing (Hermes Conduit Notifier 0.5 or later with a Conduit version that supports it), Conduit creates a secret for that pairing on your device and sends it to your Hermes gateway over your own dashboard connection, never through the relay. The plugin then encrypts notification titles, bodies, session identifiers, profile names and decision content with it, and your device encrypts clarify answers. The relay and Apple can't read that content; they still process the event type, opaque identifiers, a keyed thread token, timing and size. A pairing that has turned on encryption does not send unencrypted content again.

The relay uses this information solely to authenticate paired installations, enforce notification preferences, prevent duplicate delivery, relay clarify answers to your gateway, troubleshoot failures, and send notifications through APNs. Apple processes the device token and notification payload under Apple's applicable privacy terms.

Optional Apple Health sharing

Conduit can share Apple Health data with your Hermes gateway so your agent can read it. It is off until you turn it on, and you choose the categories (activity, heart, sleep, workouts, body measurements, vitals, fitness) and how far back to share. Conduit reads Health data only with your permission in the system Health prompt and never writes to Health.

  • Health data goes from your device directly to the Hermes gateway you configured, over your dashboard connection. It never passes through the Conduit notification relay or any server operated by Conduit, and Conduit's developer never receives it.
  • Conduit encrypts every upload end to end with a key that your device and your gateway agree on, in addition to any HTTPS, so it can only be read on your gateway.
  • By default Conduit shares hourly and daily totals, nightly sleep summaries, sleep stages and workouts. Individual readings are shared only for categories where you turn on detailed readings.
  • Your agent can read this data when it answers you, which means it is sent to the AI model provider your Hermes agent uses (and, for a hosted agent, to its host). Their privacy terms apply to what they receive.
  • If you also let a voice mode read your health data in Conduit's Settings > Health, a live voice call in that mode (Gemini Live, Grok Live or GPT-Live) can read it too: your device fetches it from your gateway, encrypted end to end, and passes what the voice model asked for to that voice provider (Google, xAI or OpenAI). Their privacy terms apply. This is off by default and separate from sharing with your agent.
  • Health data is not used for advertising, marketing or data mining, is not sold, and is not stored in iCloud. Conduit keeps only sync progress on your device, excluded from backups.
  • You can turn sharing off at any time and delete the copy on your gateway from Conduit's Health settings.

Optional location sharing

Conduit can share your location with your Hermes gateway so your agent can use it, for example to tell how far you are from an appointment, follow rules you set for places such as home or work, or not call you at places you choose. It is off until you turn it on, with two separate settings: sharing while you talk to your agent (a location when you send a message or start a voice call) and sharing in the background (arriving at and leaving places you save, and significant moves). For each you choose the precision: place names only, approximate area, or precise.

  • Location goes from your device directly to the Hermes gateway you configured, over your dashboard connection, encrypted end to end with a key that your device and your gateway agree on. It never passes through the Conduit notification relay or any server operated by Conduit, and Conduit's developer never receives it.
  • When your agent asks for your current location, the relay sends your device a silent request through Apple's push service. The request contains no location; your device answers your gateway directly, and only while background sharing is on and not paused.
  • Your saved places stay on your device. Your gateway receives their names and how far you are from them, not their coordinates. Places you mark private are reported only as "a private place".
  • Your agent can read your location when it answers you, which means it is sent to the AI model provider your Hermes agent uses (and, for a hosted agent, to its host). Their privacy terms apply to what they receive. Conduit shows you when your agent read your location.
  • Your gateway keeps location history for the period you choose (7 days by default). Location is not used for advertising, marketing or data mining, is not sold, and is not stored in iCloud.
  • You can pause or turn off sharing at any time and delete the copy on your gateway from Conduit's Location settings.

Optional Calendar, Reminders, Focus and phone status sharing

Conduit can share your calendars, reminders, which Focus is on and a snapshot of your phone (battery, charging, Low Power Mode, CarPlay, audio route, network type and motion activity such as walking or driving) with your Hermes gateway so your agent can use them. Each is off until you turn it on, and you choose which calendars and lists, how far back and ahead, and whether event notes and attendees are included. Conduit reads calendars, reminders and motion only with your permission in the system prompts.

  • This data goes from your device directly to the Hermes gateway you configured, over your dashboard connection, encrypted end to end with a key your device and your gateway agree on. It never passes through the Conduit notification relay or any server operated by Conduit, and Conduit's developer never receives it.
  • Event notes, attendee names and links are shared only when you turn on "Include notes and attendees", and attendee email addresses only when you also turn that on. Your gateway discards them otherwise. Only the latest phone status and Focus are kept, never a history.
  • Your agent can ask Conduit to add or change events and reminders. Conduit asks you before each change unless you let it add new items without asking; changes, deletions and anything on a shared calendar or list always ask. To tell your phone that a change is waiting, your gateway sends a notification through the relay, encrypted like other notifications when end-to-end encryption is on; the change itself comes from your gateway.
  • Your agent can read this data when it answers you, which means it is sent to the AI model provider your Hermes agent uses (and, for a hosted agent, to its host). Their privacy terms apply to what they receive.
  • This data is not used for advertising, marketing or data mining and is not sold.
  • You can turn sharing off at any time and delete the copy on your gateway from Conduit's Calendar & phone settings.

Retention and deletion

On-device information remains until you remove it, sign out, disable the related feature, or uninstall the app. Notification registrations remain active until notifications are disabled, the registration is invalidated, or deletion is requested. Disabling notifications prevents further delivery and revokes the app's local notification credential. Short-lived pairing codes expire after ten minutes, and event identifiers used to prevent duplicate delivery expire after approximately 24 hours. Clarify decision records, including questions, choices, and submitted answers, expire after approximately two hours. When the relay processes state, it removes expired records from its in-memory state; an expired record may remain in the saved file until a later state update rewrites that file.

To request deletion of notification relay information, contact support@milim.dev. We may ask for limited installation information to locate the registration. Information stored by your Hermes gateway must be deleted through that gateway's operator.

Sharing

Information is shared only with service providers necessary to provide the requested function, including Apple for APNs delivery and infrastructure providers used to operate the notification relay and this support site. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

Security

Conduit uses HTTPS and secure WebSocket connections when communicating with configured services. Notification credentials are randomly generated, scoped, revocable, and stored as cryptographic hashes on the relay where applicable. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Hermes Conduit is a developer and productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International processing

The notification relay and its service providers may process information in countries other than your own. By enabling notifications, you request this processing to provide the feature.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Conduit changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
